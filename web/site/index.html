Welcome to Sievepad

Write and test Sieve email filters against real messages, powered by the Sieve interpreter of Stalwart Mail Server.

  • Everything runs in your browser. Scripts are compiled and executed locally with WebAssembly.
  • Nothing is stored on a server. Your scripts, messages and settings are saved only in this browser.
  • Sharing is up to you. A share link carries the workspace inside the link itself; nothing is uploaded.

Share workspace
